Tropical Rainforest CATALOG #CANOPY-001

Monstera Deliciosa

Monstera deliciosa Liebm.

Iconic Swiss Cheese plant featuring magnificent fenestrated emerald leaves engineered to filter tropical canopy sunlight.

🌍 Natural Origin

Dense rainforests and tropical riverbanks of southern Mexico and Central America.

✨ Main Purposes

Indoor air scrubbing, ambient humidity modulation, and dramatic biophilic architectural styling.

💧 Watering

Hydrate every 7–10 days when top 50% of potting mix dries out. Mist aerial roots regularly.

☀️ Sunlight

Dappled, bright indirect sunlight (1,200–2,000 FC). Avoid harsh midday sun on glass.

🌱 Substrate

Chunky aroid substrate: orchid bark, pumice, perlite, and coco coir.

Humidity Req. 65% – 85%
Opt. Temperature 20°C – 28°C
Growth Rate Moderate
Toxicity Profile Insoluble Oxalates

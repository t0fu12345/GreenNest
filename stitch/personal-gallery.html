Subtropical Highlands

Red Rose

Rosa damascena
🌍 Geographic Origin & Natural Habitat

Temperate highland valleys of Central Asia & Ancient Persia, later naturalized across Mediterranean microclimates.

✨ Primary Botanical Purposes

Prized ornamental centerpiece, therapeutic natural aromatherapy, and traditional distillation of rose floral hydrosols.

🌿 Cultivation & Tending Directives
💧 Hydration Cadence
Deep watering 1–2 times weekly when top 2 inches dry. Water root zone; keep foliage dry.
☀️ Light Exposure
6+ hours direct daylight daily. Optimal 18°C–26°C with constant fresh air circulation.
🌱 Soil & Substrate
Rich loamy compost with perlite aeration, maintaining slightly acidic pH 6.0–6.8.
